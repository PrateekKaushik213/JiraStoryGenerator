# Community Library App — Requirements

## 1. Overview
Members of a small community library should be able to browse the catalogue, reserve books, and get reminders online. Librarians manage the catalogue and loans.

## 2. Functional requirements
- FR-1: Members sign up with email and password and can reset a forgotten password.
- FR-2: Anyone can search the catalogue by title, author or ISBN and filter by availability.
- FR-3: A logged-in member can reserve an available book; the reservation is held for 48 hours.
- FR-4: Members receive an email reminder 2 days before a loan is due and when it is overdue.
- FR-5: Librarians can add, edit and retire books, including uploading a cover image.
- FR-6: Librarians can check books out and in by scanning the ISBN barcode.

## 3. Non-functional requirements
- NFR-1: Search results return in under 1 second for a catalogue of 50,000 books.
- NFR-2: The site meets WCAG 2.1 AA.
- NFR-3: Member personal data is stored in the EU.
